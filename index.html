EspaLuz · for families building a life in Latin America

Live your new country in Spanish, with your family

EspaLuz helps families who moved to a Spanish-speaking country handle real life in Spanish: the words people really say, the customs nobody explains, and the everyday moments from the hospital and public offices to the school gate, across 21 Spanish-speaking countries. While you handle the day, Pere the sloth and Titi the monkey keep the kids learning.

What can my child do in the EspaLuz classroom?

  • Talk: your child speaks in Spanish or English, and Pere and Titi listen and answer in their own voices.
  • Trace letters, numbers and short sentences with a finger or a pen, then say them out loud.
  • Show a photo of a toy, a drawing or lunch, and the heroes turn it into the lesson.
  • Remember: sentences your child says come back in later lessons.

How does EspaLuz help parents at the doctor or a public office?

  • A voice interpreter for appointments on WhatsApp and Telegram: say it in English, it says it in Spanish.
  • Send a photo of a school note, a form or a sign and get it explained.
  • Guides for everyday moments such as the doctor, public offices, school and the bank, where EspaLuz has a guide for your country.
  • A report after your child's lessons, so you know what was practised.
